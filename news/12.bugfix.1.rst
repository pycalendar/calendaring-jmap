``ical_to_jscal`` no longer emits a ``sendTo`` property on participants, and now emits ``calendarAddress`` instead. Cyrus rejects any participant with ``sendTo`` present, on both create and update, and separately requires ``calendarAddress``, which is not one of RFC 8984's own Participant properties. Stalwart accepts either shape. Any event with ATTENDEE or ORGANIZER previously failed outright against Cyrus.
