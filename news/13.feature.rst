Added ``get_availability`` on both ``JMAPClient`` and ``AsyncJMAPClient``, returning busy intervals for one or more accounts over a time period. For your own account, it tries ``Principal/getAvailability`` first (draft-ietf-jmap-calendars, section 2.2), using your own Principal id straight from the session with no extra round trip. The issue that requested this named the primary method as ``CalendarEvent/free``, which does not exist in the spec; ``Principal/getAvailability`` is the real one, confirmed by reading the spec directly and by testing it live against both Cyrus and Stalwart.

For any other account, and for your own account if it lacks ``Principal/getAvailability`` support, ``get_availability`` falls back to a ``CalendarEvent/query`` scan with recurrence expansion, so a recurring event contributes one interval per occurrence rather than one for the whole series. Checking someone else's availability by email is not supported yet, the same limitation ``share_calendar`` already has for Principal ids in general.
