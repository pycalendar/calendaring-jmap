:mod:`convert`: JSCalendar and iCalendar conversion
===================================================

.. meta::
   :description: ical_to_jscal and jscal_to_ical, the standalone iCalendar/JSCalendar conversion functions, usable without a JMAP connection.

``ical_to_jscal`` and ``jscal_to_ical`` convert between iCalendar text and JSCalendar dicts directly, with no JMAP client or network connection involved.
